How We Work

Our structured 4-step facilitation process ensures clear communication and compliant transactions.

01

Buyer Inquiry

Buyer submits corporate specifications, required volume, target port/incoterms, and a formal Letter of Intent (LOI) / ICPO.

02

Qualification

Our desk verifies buyer readiness, credentials, and destination port feasibility against refinery compliance.

03

Introduction

We introduce qualified buyers directly to the refinery-appointed sales representative or export desk.

04

SPA & Logistics

The buyer and refinery negotiate commercial terms directly, execute the SPA, and perform transaction banking.
